# ADLC resources

While struggling to define what I actually mean by ADLC and totally thinking I personally came up with the abbreviation, formally this being AI-DLC, I think this might have been coined by some other person as well 😊 I stumbled around a couple of resources that are actually quite interesting. I would like to integrate them and read them bit by bit, and then decide if the current body of work that I have actually should contain these in part or in total. I think there are shared resources as well. 

For instance, you have the manifesto and the IBM page on the ADLC, and the loop they show there is pretty similar. There are some inner and outer loops that may be different, or terms are different, but the basic idea is the same.

What seems to be missing from my perspective is the way that features come in from the outside. This could be customers. In the Steve Yegge article "Shape of Things to Come, part one" (see the reading list) this is called the wish factory. Basically, customers and support have a thing to talk to when they just add wishes, and these enter the loop. 

Customers could mean project managers, too. This is a thing where feature requests / change requests come in from the outside. There's also an element maybe missing here, a gate. Steve Yegge talks about this as well: "There is a lot of taste involved". This pertains to architecture and feature selection: we will do it this way or that way, and no, we won't implement this feature because reasons.
Finally, there's the observation of the system: how it behaves on the load, what errors pop up, automatic triaging, automatic bug fixes, and all these kinds of things. I think these are actually included in the observation part of the ADLC.

What I would like to do is go through the resources step by step, decide what's coming in and whatnot, and create separate log entries for each resource.
