# Society

Adoption beyond the workplace: schools, labor, policy, and public understanding.

The public aspect of [`adoption/`](../). Organizations are in [`business/`](../business/), my own
path in [`journey/`](../journey/).

## Scope

- Education: what to teach when the answer is always available, and assessment under AI assistance.
- Labor: displacement, augmentation, and the distribution of both across roles and regions.
- Policy and regulation: what is being written, what is enforceable, what is theater.
- Public literacy: what non-practitioners believe about AI, and where those beliefs come from.
- Ethics: attribution, consent, environmental cost, concentration of capability.
- Culture: how norms around authorship, effort, and expertise shift.
