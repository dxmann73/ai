# Journey

My own path into agentic development: what changed, when, what forced each change, and what it cost.

The individual aspect of [`adoption/`](../). Organizations are in [`business/`](../business/), the
public in [`society/`](../society/). How the loop itself works belongs in [`aglc/`](../../aglc/);
what a tool is and how it behaves belongs in [`tools/`](../../tools/) — a note here says why a tool
was picked and what adopting it actually took.

## Scope

- The dated reconstruction of the path, and the phases it falls into.
- What triggered each transition: a failure, a tool, or an article.
- Failure modes hit along the way, named and dated.
- Adoption records for individual tools: what was chosen, how it was rolled out, what it replaced.
